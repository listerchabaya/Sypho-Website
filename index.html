Common questions.

What is a LIMS?

A laboratory information management system tracks samples, tests, results, stock, staff and documents in one place, so a laboratory can run without paper.

Can SYPHO LIMS be tailored to our laboratory?

Yes. It is modular, so you start with the modules you need, and it can be tailored to the specific workflows of any laboratory. It supports methods such as Fire Assay, AAS, XRF, ICP, gravimetric and volumetric.

Where is it hosted?

On your own servers, in the cloud, or as a hybrid of both.

Is there a low-cost option?

Yes. OpenSourceVLIMS is a free, open-source LIMS with zero licensing fees. It has been successfully installed at ZimLabs.

Do you build software outside laboratories?

Yes. We develop application software for mining, logistics, property management, finance and agriculture, including procurement, HR management, payroll and equipment management systems. We also install VPNs, build websites and provide marketing, hosting and consulting.

What happens after go-live?

Staff are trained before go-live, and we keep supporting your team afterwards.
