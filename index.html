About us

Laboratory software, made by people who know the lab.

Sypho Technologies Private Limited is a Zimbabwean technology company, founded in 2021 and registered with CIPZ on 12 June 2025. We build LIMS, enterprise software and secure networks that help laboratories across Africa work without paper.

Our Mission

To empower laboratories across Africa with innovative, reliable and affordable digital solutions that enhance productivity, ensure compliance and drive scientific excellence.

Our Vision

To go international and become the leading provider of laboratory digital transformation solutions across Africa and beyond.

  • CollaborationWorking closely with clients on their unique challenges.

Products and services

A full suite for laboratories and scientific organisations. Start with what you need and add more as you grow.

SYPHO LIMS

Our flagship, modular, web-based LIMS. Deploy on-premise, in the cloud or as a hybrid. Built for internal and commercial labs that need deep customisation.

OpenSourceVLIMS

A free, open-source LIMS with zero licensing fees. A good fit for budget-conscious labs, pilot projects and community deployments.

Customer Portal

A self-service web portal where clients request tests, track orders and download certificates of analysis.

Enterprise software

Custom software for mining, logistics, property management and finance.

Website development

Responsive, SEO-friendly websites for labs and consultancies, with a simple CMS, analytics and optional LIMS integration.

VPN and remote access

Site-to-site and remote-user VPN with AES-256 encryption, multi-factor authentication, access policies and full connection logs.

Inside SYPHO LIMS

Seven modules that share one database, so a sample, its reagents, its analyst and its client are always connected.

Methods supported: Fire Assay, AAS, XRF, ICP, gravimetric, volumetric and other standard laboratory methods. Reporting and dashboards export to CSV and PDF.
